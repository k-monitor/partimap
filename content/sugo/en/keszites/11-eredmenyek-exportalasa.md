# Viewing and exporting results

The map elements submitted by respondents can be accessed in several ways.

## Sending to your own map

If respondents have already submitted drawn elements to a map, you can export them to your own map with the **Send features to a map** link next to the sheet in the list on the survey data sheet; there you can browse and edit them, or download them in .kml format for external use.

## XLSX report

You can get the report by clicking the Surveys menu and then clicking next to the name of the survey. The exported spreadsheet contains:

- the submission ID,
- the coordinates,
- the names given,
- the text answers,
- the related attributes.

## Viewing submitted elements

With the 👁 icon next to the sheets on the survey data sheet, the submitted elements can be:

- browsed on a map,
- filtered,
- categorized,
- exported.

> 💡 **Tip:** When exporting, only the currently visible, filtered elements are included in the file.

> ⚠️ **Important:** deleting elements in the viewer does not change the responses originally stored in the survey.
