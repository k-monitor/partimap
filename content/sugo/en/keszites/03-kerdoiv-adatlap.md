# Survey data sheet

You can open a survey's data sheet by clicking the survey's name in the Surveys menu, or by creating a new survey. Here you can manage the survey's settings and edit the questions and related content.

The data sheet consists of three main parts:

- General details and settings → managing the survey's basic details
- Sheets → editing questions, pages and content
- Quiz mode → with quiz mode, you can stop respondents at a specific sheet, so the survey can be used as a live quiz or as a jointly guided activity.

The general details and settings include the following options:

1. entering the survey title;
2. the survey interface language
3. the option to edit the embed code
4. editing the custom URL;
5. setting up password protection to restrict who can respond;
6. setting a custom Facebook preview text and image;
7. entering the contact details of the data controller;
8. setting a custom thank-you message and redirect link;
9. turning the share buttons (Facebook, X/Twitter, email) on and off;
10. requesting hourly or daily email notifications about new submissions.

> ⚠️ **Important:** If you don't want to receive any more responses, you can simply deactivate the survey by setting a password.

> 💡 **Tip:** The survey title appears not only in the admin interface, but also in the browser tab title and in the preview of social media shares.

With email notifications, you can automatically keep track of new submissions as they come in.

With the thank-you page shown at the end of the survey, you can also direct respondents to further content or websites.
