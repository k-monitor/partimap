# Sheets

A survey is divided into sheets. The sheets hold the questions, informational texts and map tasks that make up the survey your respondents see.

Sheets can be managed on the survey data sheet, below the general settings. When you create a new survey, the system automatically creates a default text sheet.

Click the **Add new sheet** button to create a new sheet and start putting together the content of your survey. All the sheets you have created can be reviewed and edited in the list of sheets.

You can change the order of the sheets with the up and down arrows, and delete sheets you no longer need by clicking the trash can icon.

Each sheet can be set to one of the following types:

- Text
- Questions
- Questions and map
- Map tasks

> ⚠️ **Important:** On the first sheet, respondents must accept the Privacy Policy.

> 💡 **Tip:** It's a good idea to use a Text sheet as the first sheet, where you can present the purpose of the survey, the organization running it, the expected time needed to complete it, and any conditions for taking part.

👁 With the **View Survey** button in the top right corner, you can open the current survey at any time and check how your saved changes look.

It's worth using the editor and the survey preview side by side, so you can check the effect of your changes right away.

### Sheet types

In the sheet editor, you can switch between the sheets of the survey with the buttons at the bottom of the screen.

Click the ↗ icon to open the public view of the sheet directly, so you can immediately check how it appears to respondents.

With the green + button on the last sheet, you can add a new sheet to the survey without having to go back to the survey data sheet.

Click the PARTIMAP logo in the top left corner to return to the survey data sheet at any time.

Changes are only stored when you click the **Save** button. Saved changes appear in the public survey immediately.
