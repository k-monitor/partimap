# Drawing on the map

In some parts of the survey, you can also place markings on the map yourself.

Always try to place your markings as precisely as possible.
If necessary, zoom in on the area you want, or record your current position by clicking the location marker icon in the right corner of the map.

For this, you need to allow your browser to share your current location.

After drawing, the following may appear:

- text questions,
- a request for an explanation,
- or additional answer fields.

## Placing a point

If you want to mark a specific place:

1. Click the 📍 pin icon.
2. Click the desired place on the map.
3. If needed, write a comment or explanation in the window that opens.

You can:

- save the marking with the **Save** button,
- or delete it with the **Delete feature** button.

You can then add more points.

## Drawing a line

If you need to mark a route or a section:

1. Click the line icon.
2. Place the starting point with a single click.
3. Add the intermediate points with further clicks.
4. To finish, double-click the end point.

<figure>
	<img src="/help/sugo/kitoltoknek-draw-line.png" alt="Drawing a line" />
	<figcaption>Drawing a line on an interactive map sheet</figcaption>
</figure>

Once you have finished drawing, you can also add a comment to the element.

You can:

- save,
- edit,
- or delete the line.

## Marking an area

If you want to mark an entire area:

1. Click the area selection icon.
2. Click to mark the boundary points.
3. Click the starting point to close the area.

<figure>
	<img src="/help/sugo/kitoltoknek-draw-polygon.png" alt="Marking an area" />
	<figcaption>Marking an area on an interactive map sheet</figcaption>
</figure>

After closing the area, you can:

- give an explanation,
- write a comment,
- and then save or delete the element.
