# Answering traditional questionnaire questions

Text and multiple-choice questions work the same way as in most online surveys.

You can answer the questions:

- with a text answer,
- with a number,
- by selecting one or more answer options,
- or by entering your own answer in the “Other...” field.

After certain answers, new related questions may also appear.

You can also delete your answer to a question by clicking the red eraser icon next to the question.

## Required questions

Questions marked with an asterisk (\*) are required.
Until you answer them, you cannot move on to the next sheet or submit the survey.

## Viewing results

The PARTIMAP application can display aggregated statistics of the answers given to individual questions. If the survey creator has enabled this, you can view the aggregated answers of previous respondents after answering.

<figure>
	<img src="/help/sugo/kitoltoknek-results.png" alt="Chart of aggregated answer statistics" />
	<figcaption>If the survey creator has set this up, respondents can view aggregated statistics of the answers given by previous respondents.</figcaption>
</figure>

In this case, the **Results** page may show:

- statistics,
- charts.

From here, too, you can go to the next page with the **Next** button.
