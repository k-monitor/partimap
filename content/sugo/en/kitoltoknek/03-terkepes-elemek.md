# Browsing and rating map elements

On some sheets, you can browse the elements drawn on the map.

## Using the map

<figure>
	<img src="/help/sugo/kitoltoknek-map-controls.jpg" alt="Visitor interactions on map sheets" />
	<figcaption>Visitor interactions on map sheets.</figcaption>
</figure>

### 1. Recording your location

Click the location marker icon in the right corner of the map to record your current position.
For this, you need to allow your browser to share your current location.

### 2. Switching between base maps

Click the **map icon** in the bottom right corner of the map to switch between base maps:

- black and white,
- satellite image,
- topographic,
- or route view.

### 3.-4. Zooming in and out

You can zoom:

- with the mouse wheel,
- with the + / – buttons,
- or with two fingers on mobile.

> 💡 **Tip:** For precise markings, it's worth zooming in on the map.

## Opening map elements

Elements may have a description and further information.

You can open them:

- by clicking the element directly on the map,
- or from the list in the sidebar.

<figure>
	<img src="/help/sugo/kitoltoknek-open-features.jpg" alt="Opening map elements" />
	<figcaption>Ways to open map elements on map sheets</figcaption>
</figure>

Elements may belong to categories, which are shown below the Search... field; you can use them to filter the elements on the map.

## Rating map elements

The survey creator may also ask you to rate the map elements.

The rating can take the following forms:

### Star rating

<figure>
	<img src="/help/sugo/kitoltoknek-rating-stars.png" alt="Star rating" />
	<figcaption>Rating options on map sheets: star type</figcaption>
</figure>

You can rate the elements with stars on a scale of up to five.

### Like / dislike rating

<figure>
	<img src="/help/sugo/kitoltoknek-rating-like.png" alt="Like / dislike rating" />
	<figcaption>Rating options on map sheets: like/dislike button type</figcaption>
</figure>

You can give the elements:

- positive 👍
- or negative 👎 feedback.

In some cases, the system may also ask for a short written explanation or comment.

## Using a mobile phone or tablet

On mobile phones and tablets, the map and the sidebar are displayed at a smaller size.

The sidebar:

- can be hidden,
- and then reopened using the buttons provided.

If you select an element in the sidebar, the **Show on map** button takes you automatically to the right place on the map.

When you click a marked element on the map, after a short delay you will see:

- its description,
- and the rating option.

On mobile, you can only move on to the next page with the button at the bottom of the opened sidebar.
