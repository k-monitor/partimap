# How do I get started with filling in the survey?

To move on from the first page of the survey, you must accept the **Terms of Use** and the **Privacy Policy**.

You can navigate between the pages of the survey with the blue **Next and Back buttons** at the bottom.

	<figcaption>You must accept the Privacy Policy to view and fill in the survey</figcaption>

> 💡 **Tip:** While filling in the survey, also pay attention to the instructions shown in the sidebar or next to individual questions.
