# What can data analysis in Excel be used for?

Even if the sample is not representative, analysis in Excel still allows you to draw many valuable conclusions.

Based on the respondents' answers, you can, for example:

- identify the most important problems,
- determine which areas need improvement,
- find out which demographic groups are the most dissatisfied with a given service,
- lay the groundwork for strategic or development decisions.

The results can also help you design targeted research.

For example, if the analysis shows that it is mainly older people who are dissatisfied with the lack of benches, it is worth reaching out to them separately and assessing their needs in more detail. In this way, survey research can play an important role not only in identifying problems but also in developing possible solutions.
