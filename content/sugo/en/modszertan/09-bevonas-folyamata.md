# The community engagement process

## 1. Setting up an operational team

- Set up a project team that has the necessary expertise, decision-making authority and network of contacts. It is worth involving representatives of different fields and, where possible, stakeholders in the planning as well.

## 2. Defining needs and goals

- Map out the most important problems, the stakeholders' needs and the room for maneuver in decision-making. Clearly define the purpose of the engagement and what decision you want to support, and don't try to tackle too many topics at once.

## 3. Identifying stakeholders

- Identify who is affected by the decision, how much of a stake they have, how they feel about the change, and how much influence they have. This helps you decide whom to prioritize reaching.

## 4. Developing an outreach strategy

- Plan which communication channels you will use to reach each stakeholder group, who needs to be actively involved, who could be partners, and who requires special attention.

## 5. Choosing the right method

- Consider what information is needed for the decision, then choose a tool accordingly (e.g. online survey, forum, interview, workshop). The questions should be clear and aligned with the goal.

## 6. Reaching participants

- Define the target group, choose the right communication channels, and make it easy to take part. Only collect personal data if it is truly necessary.

## 7. Communication and implementation

- Inform stakeholders about the purpose, process and duration of the engagement. Use several communication platforms (website, social media, newsletter, on-site information) to reach as many stakeholders as possible.

## 8. Timing and tracking goals

- Set measurable goals in advance (e.g. number of respondents, target groups reached), meet deadlines, and allow enough time for participation. The process should be well scheduled and transparent.

## 9. Analyzing and presenting the results

- Process the opinions received, prepare a clear, easy-to-understand summary, then present the results to decision-makers and participants. Highlight the key lessons and the next steps.

## 10. Developing a culture of participation

- Evaluate the process, draw lessons from the experience, thank participants for their contribution, and build long-term relationships. The goal is not only a successful engagement, but also encouraging future participation.
