# Culture of participation

A successful participatory process depends not only on having the right tool, but also on whether participants trust the process and feel that their opinion really matters.

PARTIMAP helps you collect residents' opinions and suggestions, but on its own it does not guarantee genuine public participation. It is the project owner's responsibility to make the engagement transparent, credible and consistent.

When designing participatory processes, it is worth keeping the following points in mind:

- Clearly state the purpose of the survey and how the responses received will be used.
- Only approach target groups that are genuinely affected by the issue.
- Always give participants feedback on the outcome of the engagement, so they can see that their opinion had an impact.
- Avoid surveying people too often or without good reason, as this can reduce their willingness to participate.
- Aim to make participation simple, understandable and accessible to everyone.

Trust is the foundation of participatory processes that succeed in the long term. If participants find that their opinions are listened to and taken into account, they are more likely to take part in future consultations as well.
