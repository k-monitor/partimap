# Participatory methodology

## Designing participatory processes

### What does stakeholder engagement mean?

Stakeholder engagement means that decision-makers give the community an opportunity to share its opinions, experiences and suggestions while a development or decision is being prepared. Genuine participation is not just about informing people or collecting opinions; it means that the feedback received actually contributes to shaping the decisions.

**PARTIMAP** is a tool that supports participatory processes: it can be used to inform the public, to collect both map-based and traditional survey feedback, and to document community ideas. The platform aims to make community planning easily accessible not only to local governments and state bodies, but also to civil society organizations, researchers and local communities.

It is important to remember, however, that a digital tool alone does not guarantee genuine participation. Successful engagement requires transparent processes, clear goals and honest communication. Participants need to know who is collecting the data, for what purpose the data is being collected, and how the opinions received will be used.

That is why the PARTIMAP methodology encourages not only creating a survey, but also carrying out a well-designed, open and transparent participatory process. The goal is not merely to collect data, but to strengthen community dialogue and support better-informed decisions.
