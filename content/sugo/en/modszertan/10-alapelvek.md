# Principles of successful community engagement

- Clear, easy-to-understand information about the purpose and framework of the process.
- Reaching every relevant stakeholder group, especially involving those who are harder to reach.
- Providing enough time and easily accessible opportunities to participate.
- Impartial, credible information and a transparent decision-making process.
- Building partnerships with community actors and organizations.
- Publicly sharing the results and reporting back on them.
- Complying with data protection rules and collecting only the data that is necessary.
- Continuously evaluating participant feedback, improving the engagement process and strengthening the culture of participation.
