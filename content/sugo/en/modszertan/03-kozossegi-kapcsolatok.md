# Community relations

One of the foundations of a successful participatory process is a good relationship with stakeholders. The better you know your target group and the communication channels available, the more likely you are to reach the people whose opinions really matter.

Before launching the survey, it is worth thinking about:

- Who are the stakeholders you want to involve?
- Through which channels can they be reached (for example, email, social media, local organizations or in-person events)?
- How can you make sure that as many stakeholders as possible hear about the survey?
- Do you have the necessary consents, and do you comply with data protection regulations?

PARTIMAP helps you collect opinions, but the key to successful engagement is well-structured communication and distribution that builds on existing community relationships. Reaching out to people personally, forming partnerships and involving local communities can significantly increase the participation rate and the quality of the feedback received.
