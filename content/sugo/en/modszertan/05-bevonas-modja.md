# How to choose an engagement method?

Choosing the right engagement method is one of the most important steps in any project. No single tool is the best solution in every situation: the choice always depends on the purpose of the project, the range of stakeholders, the time and resources available, and the nature of the decision to be made.

In participatory processes, a combination of several methods often leads to the best results. A project might start with an information campaign, followed by an online or in-person survey, after which workshops, public forums or joint planning sessions can help prepare the decisions.

PARTIMAP primarily offers an effective way to collect large numbers of opinions and map-based feedback. With it, participants can not only answer questions but also mark problems, assets, development proposals or their favorite routes on the map. The data collected this way clearly show where similar opinions or differing needs emerge in a given area.

It is important to keep in mind, however, that a map-based survey alone rarely provides a definitive answer to a complex question. Rather, it helps uncover problems, identify conflicts, and lay the groundwork for further consultations and decisions. PARTIMAP is therefore especially useful in the early stages of planning processes, but its results can also be valuable during implementation and later evaluation.

The most successful participatory projects are those that deliberately build different engagement methods on one another. PARTIMAP is a flexible and easy-to-use component of these processes, allowing the community's knowledge and local expertise to feed into decision-making in a transparent way.
