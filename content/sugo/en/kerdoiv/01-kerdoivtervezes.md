# Questionnaire design

## Why is a good questionnaire important?

The questionnaire is one of the most important tools of communication between the researcher or project owner and the respondent. A well-designed questionnaire allows respondents to understand the questions easily and give accurate answers, and provides the organizers with reliable data for their decisions. A poorly worded or overly long questionnaire, on the other hand, can easily lead to misunderstandings, incomplete responses and biased results.

In PARTIMAP, surveys are often used to collect residents' opinions, development needs, transport problems, community planning proposals or map markings. For these, it is especially important that the questions are simple, logical and understandable to everyone.
