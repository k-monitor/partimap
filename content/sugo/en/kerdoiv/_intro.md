# How to write a questionnaire

**Help with designing your questionnaire**

A well-designed questionnaire is essential for obtaining reliable and useful results. PARTIMAP lets you combine traditional survey questions with map tasks, so you can collect opinions, experiences and location-based information in a targeted way. The guide below walks you through the most important steps of questionnaire design: from defining your research objectives and choosing the right question types, through structuring the questions logically, to taking the respondent experience into account.
