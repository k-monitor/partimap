# Why is public consultation important?

In local development projects, involving residents is often a mere formality, or it doesn't happen at all. As a result, decisions may be made that do not meet the needs of the local community and may later lead to conflict or resistance.

It often happens that civil society only joins the process once the decisions have already been made and are hard to change.

Community mapping, by contrast, makes it possible for:

- residents' views to be heard as early as the planning phase;
- local problems and needs to become visible;
- decision-makers to base their decisions on better-founded information.

## The role of PARTIMAP

PARTIMAP is a free, open-source, easy-to-use and customizable application for creating map-based surveys.

Its advantages:

- 📱 it is convenient to use on both mobile phones and computers;
- 🗺 it supports map-based answers;
- 📝 it also handles text and traditional question types;
- ⚙️ it can easily be customized for different projects;
- 🚀 it enables fast and user-friendly data collection.

PARTIMAP can be a useful tool for, among others:

- local governments,
- planning and development organizations,
- civil society organizations,
- any community that wants to involve stakeholders in decision-making.

A detailed guide to data collection methods is available, based on the DIY Toolkit and PARTIMAP's Hungarian-language documentation. And if you'd like to try out the system, it's worth filling in the demo survey too.
