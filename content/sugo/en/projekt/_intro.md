# How to plan a PARTIMAP project

**Help with planning PARTIMAP projects**

A successful map-based questionnaire survey starts with a carefully planned project. PARTIMAP lets you combine traditional questionnaire questions with map tasks, so you can collect targeted feedback about a specific area or topic. The guide below walks you through the most important steps of project planning: from defining your goals and putting together the questionnaire to choosing the map tasks and launching the survey.
