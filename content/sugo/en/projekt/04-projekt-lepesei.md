# The steps of a PARTIMAP project

A successful PARTIMAP project is built on careful planning. The more precisely you define your goals and research questions, the more useful the results you will get from the questionnaire.

During the project, it's worth following these steps:

1. Defining precise needs and goals
2. Identifying the target group
3. Putting together the questions and planning their order
4. Building the questionnaire in PARTIMAP
5. Distributing the questionnaire
6. Analyzing the data
7. Presenting the results and advocacy
