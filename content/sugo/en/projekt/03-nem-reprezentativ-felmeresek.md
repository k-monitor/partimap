# What are non-representative surveys good for?

In most cases, PARTIMAP questionnaires count as non-representative data collection. This means that the makeup of the respondents does not necessarily reflect the entire local population accurately.

However, this does not mean that the results are not valuable. Non-representative surveys can provide a great deal of information that supports planning and community dialogue.

## What can they be used for?

#### Identifying the interested community

The questionnaire can reveal who genuinely cares about the topic. These people can later be involved in further stages of the project as well, for example in forums, workshops or community planning processes.

#### Exploring the diversity of opinions

Even a non-representative survey can clearly show the different viewpoints, problems or development ideas that emerge around a local issue. This information often sheds light on aspects that would not come to the surface in a traditional consultation.
